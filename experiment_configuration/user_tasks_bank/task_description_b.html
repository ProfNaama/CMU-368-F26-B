Your tasks

In this study you complete three short tasks together with the AI assistant in the chat below: a writing task, a coding task, and a visual design task. Please read this page before you start.

How it works
  • The chat is below the three tasks. Type your messages in the chat box and click Send.
  • You may collaborate with the AI in any way you like. You can ask it questions, ask it for help, or ask it to change its work.
  • Please use only this AI assistant. Do not use other AI tools or websites.
  • Complete the tasks in order: Task 1, then Task 2, then Task 3.
  • You have 10 minutes for each task. Each task has a timer. When the time ends, finish the task and go to the next one, even if the result is not perfect.
  • To finish a task, make sure the chat shows the complete final version of your result. Then send a message with only the word FINAL.
  • Click End Chat only after you finish all three tasks. You cannot return to the chat after you click it.
Task 1 of 3: Writing

Write a response of 150 to 250 words to this question:

Is competition beneficial or harmful?

Your goal is to create a concise and persuasive argument that effectively supports your position. You may collaborate with AI. You have 10 minutes to complete the task.

To finish: make sure the chat shows the complete final text. Then send the message FINAL.

Task 2 of 3: Coding

Write a Python function that solves this problem:

Write a function that takes a list of words and returns the word that appears most often. Treat upper and lower case as the same word. If two or more words tie, return whichever one appears first in the list. If the list is empty, return None.

Name the function most_common_word. You may collaborate with AI. You have 10 minutes to complete the task.

To finish: ask the AI to show the complete final function in one message. Then send the message FINAL.

10:00

Task 3 of 3: Visual Design

Create one graphic that presents this information:

A fictional community survey asked 100 residents how they commute to work. It found that:
  • 60% used a gas vehicle
  • 20% used an electric vehicle
  • 10% used a bike
  • 10% used another unspecified method of transport

Your goal is to create a clear and visually pleasing design that communicates the information to a general audience. You may collaborate with AI. You have 10 minutes to complete the task.

To finish: when the chat shows the graphic you want to keep, send the message FINAL. Then click End Chat below the chat.
